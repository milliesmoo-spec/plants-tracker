      About Recipes

      ×

      Recipes allow you to save a list of plants that you use together in a dish.

      When you add a recipe to your weekly list, all plants in that recipe are added (duplicates are automatically skipped).

      Recipes persist until you delete them.

      About Custom Plants

      ×

      Custom Plants are plants you add that aren't in the predefined list.

      Once added, they appear in the autocomplete list under "Custom Plants" category and persist across sessions.
